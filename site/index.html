CrossInk-Indic Font Converter

Turn a .ttf into .cpfont files for the CrossInk-Indic firmware, with the OpenType shaping data its on-device shaper needs for conjuncts, reph and vowel signs.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your fonts are never uploaded.
